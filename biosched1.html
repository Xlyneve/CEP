How it works

Standard schedules are built into the planner for safer planning.

Ticking Dose 1, Dose 2, or Dose 3 means that dose has already been given.
Enter the actual date for each recorded dose. Missing or inconsistent dates require review; the planner does not invent dates.
Planning logic
1. Live vaccines are prioritised early.
2. Longer multi-dose series are also pushed earlier so treatment is not delayed unnecessarily.
3. Standard vaccine spacing is built into the planner logic.
4. The planner tries to bundle vaccines into the fewest visits possible.
5. Each visit is capped at 4 vaccines by default.
6. PCV13/PPV23 timing uses recorded dates. Booster recalls depend on age at first PPV23 and the 65th birthday. Funding labels follow the factsheet; verify individual eligibility. Calendar-month schedules are rounded forward to the next available visit. Delayed hepatitis B/HPV second doses retain a conservative four-month gap before dose 3; clinician-reviewed catch-up schedules may differ.
